# Transparency with appropriate privacy

EXIT aims to make its product claims, delivery state and future project finances
inspectable while protecting personal information, credentials and confidential
research. A pseudonymous project identity does not make public wallet activity
untraceable.

## Product reporting

Distinguish a public feature, a restricted alpha, a private prototype, a tested
mechanism and a planned feature. A model opinion is not a verified finding. A
synthetic test is not a real chain transaction. A faithful replay is not proof that
the original search covered every relevant event.

## Financial reporting after a real launch

Where relevant, publish dated periods that separately identify actual service
receipts, actual operator revenue from the service and, if any, from the token's
trading venue, operating expenses, reserves and treasury movements.
Show planned budgets separately from realized spending. Explain material changes
and attach suitable transaction references or records without exposing secrets.

No revenue amount, allocation percentage, guaranteed fee stream or treasury lock is
invented in this documentation. Those details must follow the accepted launch
configuration and subsequent real activity.

## Research and data permissions

The public website's authored notes and selected source references have a narrower
scope than the private raw-evidence archives. Permission to share a case summary
does not grant permission to share every underlying response or replay package.

Publicly accessible third-party material can still have separate reuse conditions.
Reports and exports disclose their own sharing scope; ambiguous raw material stays
restricted until its treatment is resolved.

## What stays private

Personal identity/contact records, credentials, wallet recovery material, original
private engineering history, restricted investigations and unreviewed exploit
details are not public documentation content. Project business contact is provided
through [support](SUPPORT.md).
