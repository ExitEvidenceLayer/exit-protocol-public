# BonkDAO, 6 July 2026: what the chain showed before the treasury transfer

EXIT Protocol · 28 October 2026 · all times UTC

On 6 July 2026, 4,426,104,450,305.966 BONK, all but 0.00033 BONK of the Bonk DAO treasury's BONK and 5.03% of BONK's supply,
left the treasury under a proposal titled "BIP # 76 - Sowellian BonkDAO". The proposal had passed under the DAO's own on-chain
rules. The governance program itself signed the transfer through the treasury's program-derived address, which has no private
key. BonkDAO put the value at about $20 million, as reported by
[The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/407343/bonkdao-loses-20-million-following-malicious-governance-proposal-attack) and
[Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/bonkdao-loses-20m-due-to-malicious-proposal/).

Terms used below:
- A realm is a DAO's top-level record in Solana's SPL Governance program.
- A slot is Solana's block counter.
- The voter-weight plugin is a separate program that turns deposited BONK into voting weight.
- The native treasury is a program-derived address of the governance program: only that program can sign for it, and it has no
  private key of its own.

We re-read the chain with one question: what was visible before the transfer, and when?

## What the chain shows

Every item below was re-read from Solana mainnet on 27 and 28 October 2026.

- **The rules.** The DAO's treasury is governed by SPL Governance account `Uq5BRkVf…` in the "Bonk DAO" realm `84pGFuy1…` (the
  program reported version 3.1.4 during BIP # 76). Its community rules:
  - a proposal passes with yes votes worth 1% of BONK's supply;
  - an approved transaction waits 0 seconds before it can run;
  - the community veto is disabled; the council can veto with 60% of its 11 tokens;
  - voting lasts five days, followed by a one-day cool-off.
- **When the rules took this shape.**
  - The zero wait and the disabled community veto have been in force since at least 11 January 2023, when a council proposal
    executed at 12:42:16 set them, together with a 10% community threshold.
  - The 1% threshold was set by a council proposal executed on 29 March 2024 at 18:48:23. It kept the zero wait and the disabled
    community veto. At that time only the council could create proposals.
  - On 2 November 2024 at 23:42:10, another executed proposal let any holder of 100 million BONK create proposals. It left the
    1% threshold, the zero wait and the veto setting unchanged.
  - We decoded all 87 proposals of this governance account. None changed these rules again before the transfer.
- **The treasury.** The BONK sat in token account `F8FqZuUK…`. Its owner, `AGkGWK1R…`, is the governance account's
  native-treasury address, so a passed proposal could move the balance with no delay.
- **The proposal.**
  - Wallet `8xxRdtz…` created BIP # 76 on 30 June at 08:25:24 (slot 429,842,335). Its on-chain description reads, in full:
    "Implement Sowellian governance, install new members and council, rebuild from the ashes, monetize holdings, stop the
    bleeding, all YES voters are eligible to receive tokens", then "1. Add Metadata" and "2. Send 4.426.104.450.305 Bonk to
    9bxWkNf3BtJ6iehq9KbX9uCWMjem4TFiPZ19T2sYJHvQ".
  - Its stored transfer moved 4,426,104,450,305.966 BONK, all but 0.00033 BONK of the treasury's balance, to an address that had
    no transaction history at that time.
  - Voting opened at 08:25:39.
- **The votes before the deciding one.**
  - Two Approve votes worth 98,137,694.99 BONK in total: 245,669.99 BONK on 1 July, and 97,892,025.00 BONK on 5 July at 05:53:40.
  - Four Deny votes worth 710,848,288.77 BONK in total.
  - One on-chain governance-chat message from a Deny voter, on 3 July at 00:54:33, reads: "no stated idea of what they'd do
    differently".
- **The deciding vote.**
  - Wallet `CyEE7oHV…` made its first transaction on 4 July at 14:20:36: it received 1 SOL from wallet `iGdFcQoy…` (a balance of
    about 517,240 SOL at the time), in a durable-nonce transaction whose nonce authority is `9nUNwT…`.
  - Over the next 14 hours it received 882,285,249,782.35 BONK in twelve transfers:
    - 351,888,203,104.41 BONK in five transfers from token account `HH85bz3q…`, owned by `CK8i4zFX…` (a system account holding
      about 62 SOL at the time), which signed together with the same nonce authority `9nUNwT…`;
    - 530,397,045,401.99 BONK in seven transfers from `6xAckK4U…`, owned by `5tzFkiKs…` (between about 998,000 and 1,040,000 SOL
      on 4 and 5 July), through its own durable nonce.
    Both sources behave like custodial exchange hot wallets. That is our inference, not a chain fact. The nonce authority
    `9nUNwT…` signed about 1,000 transactions an hour in the hour before the deciding wallet's first transaction, and still does
    (both measured on 28 October), which fits shared withdrawal infrastructure.
  - After borrowing about 1.67 billion BONK on a lending program and repaying 193.55 BONK more five minutes later, it held
    882,285,249,588.80 BONK.
  - On 5 July at 04:46:03 it deposited that amount through the DAO's voter-weight plugin. That was 1.003% of supply.
  - At 06:56:22 it voted Approve with exactly that weight. This vote took the proposal from failing to passing, 1 hour 29 minutes
    before the five-day voting period ended. A one-day cool-off followed; under the program's documented rules, no new Approve
    votes are allowed during it.
- **The finish.**
  - Voting closed on 6 July at 08:25:39, when the cool-off ended.
  - FinalizeVote ran at 08:26:03, signed by the proposer `8xxRdtz…`, which also signed the four executions that followed. It
    recorded the result:
    - bar: 879,947,133,976.03 BONK (1% of the full supply);
    - yes: 882,383,387,283.79 BONK, from three votes;
    - deny: 710,848,288.77 BONK.
  - Yes exceeded the bar by about 2.44 billion BONK (0.28% of the bar). Without the deciding vote, yes would have been
    98,137,694.99 BONK, 0.00011% of supply.
  - The council's veto (60% of its 11 tokens) was not used: the proposal's veto weight stayed 0.
  - The proposal's four stored transactions ran from 08:26:17: two calls labelled "Add Metadata" to a program whose purpose was
    not established here, one that opened a BONK token account for the receiving address, and the transfer. The transfer is in
    slot 431,127,240 at 08:26:28, 49 seconds after voting closed.
- **Afterwards.**
  - At 08:45:31, 19 minutes after the transfer, the deciding wallet withdrew its vote weight and its 882.29 billion BONK from the
    plugin. It then sent 190 billion BONK (08:58:38) and 112.285 billion BONK (09:44:20) to token account `HNeBzMog…` (owner
    `9hKaJu8v…`), and 580 billion BONK (09:42:47) to `AnGnd7ZQ…` (owner `8rYQBETH…`).
  - The receiving address sent 40 billion BONK to token account `JBgYJ6bZ…` (owner `Dd4vDG5v…`) between 17:00 and 17:19, and the
    remaining 4,386,104,450,305.97 BONK to token account `GAV3vVPQ…`, the treasury of a realm named "BONK 2.0" (`EbQXZCHw…`),
    created at 18:19:23 that day (last transfer 18:29:07).
  - At 18:19:31 a transaction signed only by the deciding wallet's key acted in "BONK 2.0". It created council records, minted
    one council token each for `CCXVySyR…` and the receiving address, and handed the mint authorities of that realm's community
    mint (`Hr1X2pcX…`) and council mint (`HFpbJQ…`) to the realm's native treasury `EXaJnmrL…`. From then until 14 August the
    council had 3 tokens: the deciding wallet, the receiving address and `CCXVySyR…`. A fourth member was added on 14 August by a
    council proposal ("Update Council Members", created by `CCXVySyR…`, approved by `CCXVySyR…` and the deciding wallet).
  - On 16 July `CCXVySyR…` created a council proposal in "BONK 2.0" titled "Execution BIP # 76" (18:27:04) and voted Approve
    (18:27:23); the receiving address cast the second vote (18:27:39), reaching 2 of the 3 council votes (25% threshold). A first
    execution attempt by the receiving address failed at 18:31:09. The execution at 18:40:31, signed by `FhYPjrH1…`, moved
    4,386,104,524,825.97 BONK to token account `Erz7rh8D…`, owned by `FhYPjrH1…`. On 28 October `Erz7rh8D…` held 0.2008 BONK.
  - On 21 July the receiving address took in 488,832.03 BONK in a swap (11:17:38), then sent 1,232 BONK (11:22:13) and 487,413
    BONK (11:23:04) to the other two wallets that had voted Approve on BIP # 76, `3kjkjH6v…` and `FQnQYaYe…`. Each amount is about
    0.5% of that wallet's vote weight (245,669.99 and 97,892,025 BONK).
  - On chain, these addresses share history:
    - the deciding wallet and the receiving address each got their first SOL from the same wallet, `iGdFcQoy…`;
    - both transfers used Solana's durable-nonce feature, which lets a transaction be signed in advance, and the same key,
      `9nUNwT…`, controlled the nonce in both; it also co-signed the transfers from `CK8i4zFX…` that funded the deciding vote;
    - both addresses are council members of "BONK 2.0", set up by the deciding wallet's key the same day.
    The first two links run through wallets that behave like an exchange's withdrawal infrastructure (`iGdFcQoy…` held about
    517,000 SOL; `9nUNwT…` signs about 1,000 transactions an hour). On their own they fit two withdrawals from the same service
    and do not show common control. The third link, the "BONK 2.0" council set up by the deciding wallet's key, is direct.
  - Who controls any of these addresses is not established here.
- **Today.** The governance rules are unchanged: 1%, 0 seconds, community veto disabled (re-read on 28 October 2026 at slot
  462,372,470). The treasury token account holds 0.00033 BONK.

## Reported, not checked by us

- BonkDAO put the value at about $20 million
  ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/407343/bonkdao-loses-20-million-following-malicious-governance-proposal-attack),
  6 July 2026; [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/bonkdao-loses-20m-due-to-malicious-proposal/), 7 July 2026). At CoinGecko's daily
  prices the BONK was worth about $21.7 million on 6 July and $19.5 million on 7 July.
- Forklog reports that BonkDAO said it had identified the exchange wallets used to buy BONK ahead of the proposal, and that
  Solscan linked the first recipient to a deposit made through a Bybit account. It also reports that Upbit and Kraken paused BONK
  deposits and withdrawals, that the proposal stated that those voting yes would receive BONK, and that, according to
  journalists, the tokens were not distributed among the voters. That was published on 7 July; the sends to the two other
  Approve voters on chain came on 21 July.
- [Hypernative](https://www.hypernative.io/insights/blog/the-bonk-dao-governance-drain-the-same-attack-reserve-already-stopped)
  (16 July 2026) describes the same 1% threshold and no timelock, a stake of about 882.3 billion BONK (about $4.09 million at the
  time), and a loss of about $20.5 million.
- [Rekt](https://rekt.news/bonkdao-rekt) (10 July 2026) says an anonymous wallet spent $4.4 million buying just over 1% of
  BONK's circulating supply, that 882.1 billion BONK was bought on Bybit and Binance over the prior two days, and that Solscan
  tags the wallet "funded by Bybit". It says seven wallets decided the vote, which matches the chain's seven votes, and that
  eighteen thousand members did not vote. It values the drained BONK at about $19.3 million.
- Rekt also puts the vote's close at 09:42 UTC and says the treasury emptied in the same block. The chain differs: voting closed
  at 08:25:39, and FinalizeVote, the executions and the transfer are separate transactions between 08:26:03 and 08:26:28.

## What this shows, and what it does not

- **The transfer followed the DAO's own rules.** One wallet's deposit of just over 1% of the supply was enough to pass a proposal
  that moved almost all of the treasury's BONK, with no waiting period. The margin was 0.28% of the bar.
- **Vote weight counted as soon as BONK was deposited, and it could be withdrawn after the vote.** That is what the plugin did
  in these transactions. We did not review the plugin's program code.
- **A low threshold or a zero waiting period is a design choice.** Recording it is not a verdict on anyone.
- **EXIT as it runs today would not have watched this governance account.** Our watches start from token mints and Ethereum
  contracts, and the BONK mint has no authority that leads here. Reading governance rules and new proposals is the gap this case
  makes concrete.
- **Not covered here:**
  - off-chain discussion;
  - the plugin's source code;
  - what the program allows during the one-day cool-off (its length was read on chain; the behaviour comes from the program's
    documentation);
  - who controls any address, including the published claims about the wallets that funded the deciding vote.
  Nothing here claims the transfer could have been stopped.
